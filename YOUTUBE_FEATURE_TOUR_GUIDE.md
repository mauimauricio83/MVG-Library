# MVG Library — Feature Tour Video Guide

A shot-by-shot guide for a YouTube walkthrough of themusicvideoguy.com. Organized as a suggested recording order, with exact button labels/paths (so you can find things fast on camera) and a talking-point angle for each beat. Skip/reorder freely — this is a checklist, not a script.

Admin-only tools (Manage Entries, admin-intake, blog editor) are intentionally left out — this is a viewer-facing tour.

---

## 0. Cold open (30–60s)

- Land on the homepage signed out. Mention the live stats line under the tagline (video/entry count).
- Hook: "Every music video, every credit, all in one place" — this is a *credits* database first, a video player second. Every entry tracks Director, Producer, DP, Editor, Choreographer, Studio — not just artist/song.
- If it's your first time in this browser session, the **Welcome Gate** modal will appear — good beat to show: "Sign in with Google" vs "Continue as Guest," followed by an optional "What do you like?" genre picker (checkboxes, skippable, changeable later in Settings) that nudges the homepage Discover section toward your picks. Browsing works fully signed out; sign-in only unlocks personalization (My Queue/Favorites/Playlists sync, Connect/Profiles, voting, comments, messaging).

---

## 1. Homepage tour (2–3 min)

Scroll top to bottom, calling out each section (this is the actual on-page order — it was recently reshuffled to put your own stuff first):

1. **Browse tabs** — By Director / By Artist / By Song Title. Explain results regroup by whichever tab is active.
2. **Filters row** — Clear filters, Genre/Year/Country dropdowns, category chips, MVG Reels-only toggle. Click one filter live to show results narrow instantly.
3. **Viewer's Choice** — top-2 highlighted + a supporting grid, driven purely by community votes (segue to §7 later).
4. **My Queue** — a straight YouTube Watch Later clone, deliberately. Point out the "+" that now lives on the bottom-right corner of *every* thumbnail across the entire site — hover, tap plus, done, no opening the video first. One-click remove (✕) right on each queued thumbnail too. My Queue's title click and its Play All both open the same place: the dedicated playlist player (see §5).
5. **Maui's Picks** — hand-curated sidebar, capped at 6, oldest evicted as new ones are added. Good "personal recommendation" beat.
6. **News** sidebar — latest blog posts pulled in automatically.
7. **Latest Submissions** — horizontally scrolling strip (Play All / arrows / "See all"), deliberately weighted toward genuinely recent adds rather than old bulk-import entries.
8. **Jump-nav row** (A–Z letters) — only letters with actual entries show up; no dead buttons.
9. **Featured strip** — separate curation lane from Maui's Picks.
10. **Discover** — infinite random browsing, "See more" keeps pulling fresh picks with no repeats in-session. This is the section your onboarding genre picks quietly weight toward — still shows everything else mixed in, never a hard filter. Good contrast to Featured/Picks: mostly chance, lightly steered by you.
11. **Main grid** — grouped by letter under the active tab, full catalog.
12. **Hub links** at the very bottom — "Browse by Director" / "Browse by Artist," dedicated SEO pages.
13. **Sidebar shortcut** (desktop only) — scroll the left sidebar down past Support!: a "My Playlists" mini-list, your top 5 playlists plus a "More" button that expands to everything. Good "you're never more than one click from your stuff" beat.
14. **Message board** — the small chat-bubble icon bottom-right of the page, on every view. A persistent, site-wide public chat, separate from per-video comments (§3). Sign-in required to post.

## 2. Search (1 min)

- Use the top-bar search. Type a partial artist/song/director name — show results filtering live, no dropdown/autosuggest, it's instant.
- Great on-camera trick: **type a misspelled or accented name** (e.g. drop an accent mark, or fat-finger a letter) and show it still finds the match — search is accent-insensitive and typo-tolerant.
- Mention it also searches producer/DP/editor/choreographer/studio/description — not just the obvious fields. Try searching a crew member's name if you have one memorized.
- Results now render two-up on wide screens instead of one long skinny list — point out the view toggle next to the A–Z row (list icon / grid icon) that swaps to a pure thumbnail grid, whichever you prefer.
- **Bulk selection**: every result card has a low-opacity checkbox in its bottom-right corner. Check a few and a "Selection" bar appears above the results — "Select all" (current results only)/"Clear," and an **Actions** menu: Add to Favorites, Add to Playlist, Save as New Playlist, Play Selected (opens the playlist player with just what you picked). Good "batch, don't repeat yourself" beat.

## 3. Clicking a video — the lightbox (2–3 min)

Clicking any thumbnail anywhere on the site (outside TV Mode) opens the **lightbox** — this is the real detail page for an entry and deserves its own beat; it's easy to undersell on camera by only showing TV Mode.

1. **Player + title row** — video embed up top, title, then a row of action buttons: Add to playlist (+), Vote, Favorite (♡), Share (copies a direct link — every entry is deep-linkable), Widen (⤢) to enlarge the player, and Report an issue.
2. **Credits block** — every tracked field (Director, Producer, DP, Editor, Choreographer, Studio, Country w/ flag, genre tags, release date) laid out clearly, not buried in a description dump.
3. **Suggest an edit** — click a credit field and a "Suggest an edit" form pops up: pick the field, see the current value, type your suggested correction and an optional reason, submit. Crowdsourced accuracy without opening admin access to everyone — good "the community helps keep this correct" beat.
4. **Description** — the submitter's own write-up, when they gave one.
5. **Download trading card** — a genuine highlight, easy to miss: below the description, a link renders a downloadable, shareable **collectible-style card** for the entry (MTG/Top-Trumps-inspired, Instagram-ready portrait format) — title, artist, director, category/year, genre tags with a color-coded border (each genre hashes to a consistent color; two genres split the border diagonally), country flag, and a pulled-from-YouTube/Vimeo art crop, all client-side, nothing pre-rendered. Hovering the link shows a live preview before you download. Great, very shareable closing shot for this section — download one on camera and show the actual PNG.
6. **Related videos** — a row of similar entries at the bottom, one click to jump straight to another lightbox.
7. **Comments** — per-video comment thread at the very bottom, sign-in required to post, public to read. Distinct from the site-wide message board (§1.14).

## 4. TV Mode — the centerpiece (5–7 min)

This is the most built-out feature and deserves the most screen time.

**Entry**: sidebar "TV Mode" or the mobile bottom-nav TV icon.

1. **Armed/static screen** — show the analog TV static (real per-frame noise, not a looping gif) before pressing play. The title/thumbnail of what's about to play is deliberately hidden until you hit play — channel-surfing feel.
2. **Power switch** — point out the physical ON/OFF toggle styling. Flip it off/on to show it drops back to the static screen.
3. **Filter side panel** (CRT-style panel on the right in windowed/4:3, minimal in 16:9 fullscreen) — four tabs:
   - **Genre** — 10 broad umbrella buckets (Pop, Rock, Metal & Punk, Hip-Hop/Rap, R&B/Soul/Funk, Electronic/Dance, Country/Folk, Latin/World/Reggae, Jazz/Blues/Classical, Other), single column.
   - **Era** — the rotary dial control. Show all three granularities: **Years** (exact), **Eras** (decade split into Early/Mid/Late), **Decades** (coarse). This dial is a genuinely unusual UI piece — worth a close-up.
   - **Custom** — tune the channel to one of your own Playlists.
   - **📻 Channel** — see next point.
4. **Channel Mode** — the standout feature: a single shared, synced broadcast everyone watching sees at the same timestamp, like a real TV channel, not per-viewer playback. Show the "You're tuned into the Channel — up next: …" status line. If comments are enabled, show the live comment panel tied to the channel.
5. **Track-change flash** — every skip/advance briefly flashes static as a transition. Point it out on a Next click.
6. **Playback controls** — custom remote-style controls, no seek bar by design: Prev / Play-Pause / Next / Mute / Volume, plus Report issue, Favorite (♡), Vote, Add-to-playlist (+), Crop-to-4:3, Widen/shrink, Fullscreen (⛶), CC captions, Share/copy-link, Info (ⓘ).
7. **OSD indicators** — mute/volume changes show classic TV-remote-style on-screen indicators (MUTE label, volume bar).
8. **4:3 crop (windowed)** vs **4:3 in fullscreen** — two independent toggles, don't combine on camera (known limitation). Show one, then the other, separately.
9. **Fullscreen** — demonstrate entering fullscreen, toggling 16:9 vs 4:3 inside it, and the auto-hiding fullscreen HUD (mouse-idle for a few seconds to show it fade, then move to bring it back).
10. Mention: YouTube's own title/channel overlay may briefly peek in on pause/load — that's YouTube's embed doing that, not something MVG Library controls (no need to dwell on it).

## 5. Favorites, Playlists, Recently Viewed (2–3 min)

- **Favorites**: tap ♡ on a couple of cards to fill it, open the dedicated Favorites page (sidebar), show Play All. Demo **Share Favorites / "Get link"** (requires sign-in) — generates and copies a public read-only link.
- **Playlists**: create one via "+ New Playlist," or show "Create Playlist" in the filters row, which snapshots your *current search results* into a playlist. New playlists land at the top of the list, not the bottom. The page is split into two clear groups: **My Playlists** (yours, newest first) and **Default Playlists** (pre-seeded, one per major director, e.g. Michel Gondry, Spike Jonze). My Queue sits fixed at the top above both, always.
- **The playlist player** — click any playlist chip (or My Queue's title/Play All) and it opens a dedicated player: a normal-looking video lightbox with the full playlist rail down the *left* side (thumbnails, song/artist, a remove button per track) — deliberately not TV Mode, no channel/static machinery, just a clean "here's your list, click through it." Prev/Next transport, a shuffle toggle for the remaining queue, and Rename/Delete live right in the player for custom playlists (hidden for My Queue and one-off "Play Selected" lists, which can't be renamed).
- **Recently Viewed**: open a few entries, then show the Recently Viewed strip auto-populating (capped at 12). Mention Settings has a one-click "Clear history."

---

## 6. Submit a music video (1–2 min)

- Sidebar "Submit music video" → walk the form: Artist/Song/Director/Category/Email/Year/Country required; YouTube or Vimeo link (need at least one); optional Description, Producer, DP, Editor, Choreographer, Studio, release date.
- Mention every submission is manually reviewed before it goes live — not auto-published — and there's a bulk-submission path (shared spreadsheet + email) for anyone submitting a large batch.
- Show the "Thanks" screen after submitting (Ko-fi link + "Submit again").

---

## 7. Voting (1–2 min)

- Click **Vote** on a card, in a lightbox, or in TV Mode — show the "Voted!" flash. Mention voting is currently free/unlimited, no cap per person.
- Open the top-bar **Vote** button → Vote modal, show the "Top Videos" leaderboard.
- Mention Settings has an opt-in "Show my name on videos I vote for" toggle (off by default) — a nice privacy-first detail to call out.

---

## 8. Connect mode / Profiles (1–2 min, optional)

- Toggle **Watch → Connect** in the sidebar — explain it's a display filter, not a separate account type; jumps straight to Profiles.
- Profiles is a director/artist/crew directory auto-populated from the credits already in the database, plus messaging/collaboration requests. Requires sign-in to browse. Good "the database works both ways" beat — credits data doubles as a real directory.
- **Direct messages**: once you've connected with someone through Profiles, a Messages panel lets you DM them directly, in-app — no email/social handoff needed.

---

## 9. Settings & account (1 min)

- Open Settings (gear icon): Dark/Light theme toggle, Autoplay on/off, Clear Recently Viewed / Clear Favorites, Share Favorites toggle, Username + "show my name on votes," sign-out.
- **Genre preferences** live here too — the same checkbox grid from onboarding, editable anytime: pick what you're into and Discover leans toward it, without ever hiding anything else.
- Mention sync: My Queue/Favorites/Playlists/Recently Viewed work instantly offline-first (localStorage), and sync across devices once signed in — no account required to use the core site.

---

## 10. Easter eggs / closing flourishes (1 min, fun closer)

- Scroll to the footer and click the small hidden **"cloud"** link → the 3D **Word Cloud** page: a draggable, zoomable sphere of artist/song/director names sized by how often they appear among recent submissions ("Drag to spin · scroll to zoom"). Great visual closing shot.
- Quick mentions if time allows: Discord community link, News blog, Podcast ("The Film School You Never Went To" — embedded Spotify player), Support/Ko-fi page.

---

## 11. Outro (30s)

- Recap: it's a real credits database (not just a video list), the TV Mode/Channel experience is the standout, every entry has its own shareable trading card, My Queue and the rest of your personal library are one click away everywhere, and everything works signed-out — personalization (My Queue/Favorites/Playlists/Voting/Connect/DMs/comments) is one Google sign-in away.
- CTA: link in description to submit a video, join Discord, support on Ko-fi.
