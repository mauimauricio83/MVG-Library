Welcome to the MVG Library

Maui Mauricio · August 8, 2026

AI Need Not Apply.


We built this database to celebrate the actual human beings behind the camera. The MVG Library currently holds over 13,000 music videos. Every single entry was made by human hands. Generative AI music videos are explicitly banned from this catalog. This space is dedicated strictly to real directors, choreographers, and the crews spending weeks in editing bays.


You can browse the entire collection right now without creating an account or hitting a paywall. The search engine lets you pull up videos by artist, director, or the specific year a track dropped.



Stop clicking around and let the music play. TV Mode turns the database into a lean-back broadcast channel. You pick your filters, flip the power switch, and the library generates a continuous shuffle queue of whatever matches. We built a 4:3 crop toggle directly into the player. Hitting that button forces widescreen videos back into a classic CRT television ratio for a pure 90s broadcast feel.

You can build your own saved Playlists or hit the heart icon on any video to curate a Favorites list. The site generates a public link so you can share your picks with anyone.

The site serves two distinct purposes. The front end is for fans to watch. The back end exists to help artists network.


Signing in with a Google account unlocks the Connect directory. This is an active, searchable network of musicians and production studios looking to collaborate. The system automatically cross-references your display name with the database's credit fields. If you directed a video in our catalog, it automatically surfaces on your profile. You can send a request to collaborate with a stranger. Once they accept, a private message thread opens up.


We added a public intake form where anyone can submit a missing video. Every submission is reviewed manually by a human. There is a site-wide message board for public chats and a rotating 3D Word Cloud tracking current submission trends. You can even install the entire library as a home-screen app on your phone.

Go hit the Discover button on the homepage. Let the randomizer run until you find a video you completely forgot existed.
