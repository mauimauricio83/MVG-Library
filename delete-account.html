Delete Your Account & Data

MVG Library doesn't require an account — you can browse, search, and use TV Mode fully signed out. Signing in with Google is optional and only used to sync your Favorites and Recently Viewed history across devices.

Clearing data yourself, right now

You can clear your Favorites and Recently Viewed history at any time, on-device and in your synced account, from the app's Settings menu — no need to contact us for this.

Deleting your account entirely

To have your account and all associated data permanently deleted, email maui@themusicvideoguy.com from the address associated with your Google sign-in, with the subject "Delete my MVG Library account." We'll confirm and complete the deletion within a few days.

This removes:

  • Your account record (name, email, profile picture received from Google Sign-In)
  • Your synced Favorites and Recently Viewed history stored in our database

Questions about this process: maui@themusicvideoguy.com
