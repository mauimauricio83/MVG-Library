An intimate piano and vocal session, captured in one take. "Broken Wings" is a 3-in-1 experience — live performance, music video, and song recording all at once (no autotune was used). The performance showcases the delicate interplay between voice and piano in a cozy historic setting in Lisbon, Portugal. "Broken Wings" delves into the emotional release and struggle of facing a manipulative character and the journey to move on.
