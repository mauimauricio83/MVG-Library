Lübodisko is a song from Elektro Hafiz which combines a fusion of Anatolian rock and psychedelia. For this song I directed and animated a 2d animation / live-action music video. In my point of view, Lübodisko is the nest for the people living under oppression. Animations illustrate the Lübodisko characters in the light of local tunes with disco touch which plants the idea of being in limbo. Those are the ones who embrace dancing as a way of expression of freedom against all kind of oppression and torment. They serve to heavy nature of struggle with their uniqueness spanning over the shades of grey. They rebel against war, racism, sexism and all kinds of injustice with their dance and souls.
