Debut single by the audiovisual collective ReVerse Bullets . . . On Halloween night, a young girl awaits her first date, only to be picked up by a Man With No Face. What follows is a hypnotic taxi ride from Brooklyn into the heart of Manhattan during the Halloween parade as the girl finds herself surrounded by costumed freaks who make her question her own identity -- is she wearing the costume or are they?
