A lifetime unfolds in fragments. An older couple find themselves drawn back into the moments that shaped their life together: a first encounter, fleeting tenderness, arguments, hardship, laughter and the quiet intimacy of simply growing old side by side. But time is not linear. Their younger selves begin to emerge within the present, creating a shifting portrait of a relationship lived twice, where memories, reality and imagination become impossible to separate. As their two lives converge during a performance by Paul The Silver, the past catches up with the present and a lifetime of love, loss and everything in between is briefly held in the same moment. Then it begins again.
