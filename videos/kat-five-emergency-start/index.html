A fierce neon-infused call to art action, Emergency Start features physical struggle, electro rave-punk music, and is inspired by anime, video games, and zine culture. With graphics and animations from Berlin-based cross media artist Tank Thunderbird, cinematic action sequences shot by London filmmaker Jenn Webb, and dramatic creative direction and costume from Claudi Panaite.
