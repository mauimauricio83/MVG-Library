Palm Pictures' "The Work of Director..." DVD series (seven volumes covering auteurs including Spike Jonze, Michel Gondry, Cunningham, Chris, Mark Romanek, Corbijn, Anton, Stéphane Sednaoui and Jonathan Glazer) collects the landmark music video filmographies of the directors who defined the form. Companion releases include Hype Williams' disc and the Hammer & Tongs collection.
