Reapers Ghost brings you a music video infusing art and real life: Angie performs alongside her childhood creation, Johnny Reaper. Johnny Reaper is the Son of the Grim Reaper, who would rather be a rockstar… And he wants to get through to me! Painkiller is about Johnny Reapers perspective: he simply asks me to open up my heart, to not wallow in troubles on your own...to let him be my painkiller. Johnny Reaper always was my painkiller. My character, saved me from many things , he was always my Painkiller. So in true , Johnny Reaper fashion, here is the chaotic , punk rock song which blends my world and his ! He is trying so desperately to pull me into his world....did he manage?.....
