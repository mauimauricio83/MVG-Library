SAJDE, written by Sheldon Bangera, is a worship song that reflects the heart of surrender, reverence, and worship before the One who is worthy. The word Sajde speaks of bowing low before God—an outward expression of an inward posture of humility, surrender, and complete dependence on Him. Through Jesus, we have encountered a love that came down to save us, redeemed us, and brought us back to the Father.
