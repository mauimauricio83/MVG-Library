The official music video of Archipelago's second single, May 1. Starring Cassandra Soenen as The Ballerina. Directed by Quark Henares. Cinematography by Pong Ignacio. Edited by Maui Mauricio. Production Manager: Adi Lopez. Filmed on location at the Ateneo de Manila University campus in cooperation with the Loyola Film Circle.
