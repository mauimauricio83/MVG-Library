Archipelago's official music video for "Gaan", the second single since the release of the award-winning, critically-acclaimed debut album Travel Advisory. Featuring frontman Yan Yuzon and the beautiful country (and people) of Thailand. Directed and filmed by Yan Yuzon, Mia Guerrero and Nina Guerrero. Shot with a Lumix LX-3. Editing and Color Correction by Maui Mauricio. Filmed entirely on location in Bangkok and Pattaya. Produced by Archipelago.
