Independent artist BoiHardy aka Hardeep Singh presents a cinematic music film directed by Jonathan Ravada, exploring heartbreak, intoxication, and self-realization through a dual-reality narrative. Built on a slow, melancholic blend of Hindi pop, RnB, and rap, the song carries a restrained emotional weight, moving between vulnerability and quiet intensity as it unfolds. Set between a moonlit jungle and a reckless night drive, the film follows a man who, through a mysterious phone call, is forced to confront a past he cannot undo. What begins as an intimate portrayal of love and loss gradually takes an unexpected turn, with the visuals reframing the song into a story of consequence and awareness. Ravada approaches the film with performance-led storytelling, using controlled camera movement and subtle disorientation to mirror the protagonist’s shifting state of mind, blurring the line between memory and reality. Shot entirely during the day and transformed into night in post-production, a visual approach led by Nitin Sharma(Director of Photography & Editor), the film uses light and color as emotional tools rather than strict realism. With this film, Hardeep and Ravada create a piece where music and visuals exist in conversation, not just complementing each other, but transforming the way the story is experienced. The release also builds momentum toward Hardeep’s upcoming album, which continues his exploration of love, identity, and emotional conflict.
