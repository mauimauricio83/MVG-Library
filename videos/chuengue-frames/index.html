Frames" projects memories like vintage film reels, utilizing an experimental, high-contrast aesthetic that blends past and present. The visual narrative alternates between slow, contemplative shots and vibrant flashes—which intensify into a rapid whirlwind as the music builds—suggesting the shedding of time’s constraints and the beauty of existential ephemerality.
