Official Music Video for ‘Big Light’ by Alice Thorn. ‘Big Light’ centres around themes of neurodivergence and the sensory experiences that come with it. Visually, the video explores visual distortions reminiscent of ‘Alice in Wonderland’, playing with size, scale and positioning. Cutting between scenes that evoke feelings of discomfort and overstimulation, and dark, comforting spaces creates a sharp juxtaposition that is both visually captivating and unsettling.
