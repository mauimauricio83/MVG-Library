A short heist film in the form of a music video. The concept of the song is about the feeling of not fitting in a space anymore because one has expanded their thinking and no longer feels they belong. The feeling that you've been plodding along, maintaining yourself inside of that space's small perspective and way of thinking.. but you've had a shift of outlook that you're ready to move on and make a break for it. I chose to illustrate this concept via motifs of the heist genre... leaning into the aesthetics of the late 60's early 70's to create a sense of retro-futuristism and a timeless feeling.
