This music video is a cinematic short film set to a powerful score, exploring the tender bond between a father and child. Through breathtaking visuals and an emotionally charged narrative, it delves into life's fleeting moments and love that transcends time. More than a music video, it is a moving portrait of family, memory, and quiet sacrifices. Every frame is crafted with feature-film care, evoking raw, universal feelings that resonate with anyone who has loved or lost. It is not just a song - it is a journey into what it means to be human.
