A Republic (If You Can Keep It) is a cinematic rock anthem by Canadian singer-songwriter BJ Spicer, inspired by Benjamin Franklin’s enduring challenge to preserve democracy. Through powerful lyrics and striking imagery, the song reflects on freedom, responsibility, and the choices that shape a nation’s future. A timeless reminder that the strength of a republic ultimately rests in the hands of its people.
