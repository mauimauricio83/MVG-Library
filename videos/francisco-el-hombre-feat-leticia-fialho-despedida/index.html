Let the last one to leave turn the lights off, but don’t close the door, after all, this isn’t farewell, it's just “see you later.” After 12 years of making music, Francisco, el Hombre has decided to hang up their instruments as a quintet and set off toward new horizons. In tribute to this moment before their “creative nap,” the band is releasing the music video for “Despedida” (Farewell).
